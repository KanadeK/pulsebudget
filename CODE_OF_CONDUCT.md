# Code of conduct

PulseBudget contributors are expected to make technical collaboration safe and useful.

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique code and evidence, not people.
- Welcome questions and different experience levels.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, reveal private information, or publish confidential
  traces.
- Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and participation that violate these
  expectations.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the repository's security contact.
Good-faith reports will be reviewed without retaliation.
